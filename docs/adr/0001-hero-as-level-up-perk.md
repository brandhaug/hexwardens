# Hero as a Level-Up Perk

Heroes (tribe-flagship Units) are not trainable with gold and do not level up through play. A Player summons their Tribe's Hero by choosing the `special_unit` Village Perk during a Village's level-up to level 5; the perk consumes that level-up's choice and is the only level at which `special_unit` appears. Captured Villages keep the Perk choice their previous owner made, so a captor cannot retroactively summon a Hero from a captured level-5+ Village.

Considered alternatives: (a) auto-spawn at level 5 (Hexwardens' apparent default) and (b) a player-triggered "Summon Hero" action with a transferable unspent slot. Both make the Hero feel like a freebie. The Perk-choice model makes the Hero a real opportunity cost — territory, +gold, +population, or Hero — and treats the level-up choice as the irreversible commitment, which composes cleanly with our "Village level and Perks survive capture" rule.
