# Daily Challenge as a passive Public Queue modifier

The **Daily Challenge** stops being its own Match shape. Today's `/daily` route — a solo-vs-bot Match on a date-seeded Board with a date-keyed tribe assignment — is replaced by an objective layered passively onto every **Public Queue** Match for the day. Daily Challenges become a first-class concept in `CONTEXT.md`, sibling to **Achievements**.

A Daily Challenge is one shared, time-boxed objective ("capture 5 Villages") that resets at UTC midnight. Every Player in every Public Queue Match that day is evaluated against it automatically; there is no opt-in, no dedicated queue, and no curated map. Completion is **independent of the Match's win/loss outcome** — once the objective triggers on a Player's Turn, the Player has completed today's daily — but a **forfeit by the completing Player voids** the result. Match-end recap surfaces the completion; the existing `daily-challenge-streak-3` Achievement continues to consume the same per-day binary signal.

Ranking is by **fewest Turns to complete**, best across the Player's Matches that day. The previous composite score (`turns × 10 + unitsLost × 5`) collapses to `bestTurns` — `bestUnitsLost` drops from the formula because losses-to-bots were the only thing it ever measured, and PQ Matches are all-human. The leaderboard moves from `/daily` (deleted) to a tab on `/leaderboard`. Today's challenge is shown as a briefing card in `/lobby` (pre-match) and as an in-match HUD element on `/multiplayer-game`. The HUD progress counter is **client-derived** from the existing `BroadcastEvent` stream — the same pure projection function lives in `packages/game-logic/src/daily-challenge/` and is the authoritative evaluator on the game-server. Progress is **private to the active Player** to avoid leaking strategic information.

The objective registry mirrors `ACHIEVEMENT_REGISTRY` in shape: each entry is a stable-ID, name, description, and a pure event-projection function returning `{ progress, target, completed, completedAtTurn }`. The active challenge for a date is selected by deterministic rotation over the registry, indexed by `dailyDateToSeed(date)` — that helper survives, even though the date-as-Board-seed and `dailyTribeAssignment` usages are deleted along with the bot-match builder. The `daily_results` table keeps `(userId, dailyDate, bestTurns, attemptCount, firstCompletedAt, lastAttemptAt)`; `bestUnitsLost` is dropped and `bestScore` becomes redundant with `bestTurns`. Pre-shift rows are non-comparable to the new shape and are wiped on the migration that drops the dead columns.

This is the first feature where in-Match logic in `packages/game-logic` is consumed not just by the Match itself, but as a sibling per-Player projection on the same event stream. It establishes the pattern for any future "objective layered onto a Match" feature — weekly challenges, tribe-specific quests, season-pass goals — without those features needing their own Match shape.

## Considered and rejected

- **Daily Challenge as a daily-flavored Achievement.** Achievements are permanent per-account unlocks identified by stable IDs; a Daily Challenge is keyed by `(challengeId, dailyDate)` and needs per-day reset semantics. Forcing it into the Achievement schema either bloats Achievement IDs with date components or breaks the "Achievement = one-time unlock" invariant.
- **Opt-in toggle at queue time.** Splits the Public Queue matchmaking pool by daily-vs-not preference. Hexwardens' player population isn't large enough to fragment the pool without elongating queue times.
- **Dedicated Daily Queue alongside Public Queue.** Same fragmentation problem, plus duplicates the matchmaker DO's responsibilities. The daily is meant to be a side-goal, not a separate competitive ladder.
- **Day-cumulative counter ("5 captures across all your PQ Matches today").** Moves the daily from "a Match goal" to "a daily grind." Loses the "today, on this Match, do this" framing. Adds D1-row counter writes per relevant event instead of per-Match-end.
- **Completion requires winning the Match.** Stacks two skill gates on top of each other; lower-skill Players almost never complete the daily. The streak Achievement is the prestige layer for serial completion — the daily itself should be approachable.
- **Curated daily Board (force-apply `dailyDateToSeed` to every PQ Match today).** Hostile to PQ Players who don't care about the daily. Map memorisation as a daily-skill was never the intent.
- **Keep `/daily` as a briefing-plus-leaderboard page with a "Play in Public Queue" CTA.** Lower-disruption but preserves an indirection (a page about a thing that no longer has a page) and a duplicated leaderboard surface. Folding into `/lobby` and `/leaderboard` is the cleaner end state.
- **Server-pushed `dailyProgress` wire message per relevant event.** Matches the fog-of-war "server-trusted, client-displays" pattern but is overkill for an in-match counter the client can derive from events it already receives. The authoritative completion check still happens server-side and is the final word at Match-end; in-Match display drift is cosmetic.
- **Opponent-visible daily progress.** Distorts opponent play — defenders cluster around Villages when the leader is at 4/5 — and reframes the daily as a head-to-head subgame the design doesn't want.
