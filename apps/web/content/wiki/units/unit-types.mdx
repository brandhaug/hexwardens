There are 10 general unit types and 9 tribe-specific [hero units](#hero-units) in Hexwardens. General units are [trained at villages](/wiki/units/training-and-healing) once their [technology](/wiki/mechanics/technology) requirements are met. Heroes are summoned only through a [Village Perk](/wiki/buildings/villages#village-perks) and cannot be trained with gold.

## General Units

Each card shows the unit's stats — ❤️ HP, ⚔️ ATK, 🛡️ DEF, 👣 MOV, 🎯 RNG, 💰 Cost — along with combat tags, passive abilities, and the [technology](/wiki/mechanics/technology) required to train it.

<WikiUnitCodex />

A capturing unit must be in **ready** mode on the [Village](/wiki/buildings/villages) tile and stays locked there until the start of its Tribe's next Turn — see [Capture](/wiki/mechanics/capture) for the full two-step flow.

### Passive Abilities

- **Brace** (Spearman) — Deals 1.5x damage when counter-attacking cavalry (Seer, Sprinter)
- **Charge** (Sprinter) — +5 ATK when attacking after moving
- **Retreat** (Seer) — After attacking and surviving, may make one follow-up move but cannot attack again that turn
- **Splash** (Bombardier, Mage) — Each hit also deals reduced damage to enemy units adjacent to the target tile
- **Tide Command** (Tidebreaker) — Strong on water and can attack after moving there

Two further passives are wired into the combat and movement engine but are not yet assigned to any unit. They land when the relevant unit is added:

- **Knockback** — Pushes the surviving target one tile away from the attacker. Blocked tiles deal +5 damage in place; non-naval units pushed onto water or ocean drown. Pushed (or drowned) targets cannot counter-attack. See [Combat](/wiki/units/combat#knockback).
- **Flying** — In transit, every tile costs 1 movement and tech gates are ignored. The destination tile must still satisfy the normal gates ([Climbing](/wiki/mechanics/technology) for stone, Water & Bomber for shallow water, Ocean & Admiral for deep ocean). Flying units gain +1 sight and default to attacking after moving. See [Movement](/wiki/mechanics/movement#flying).

## Unit Roles

**Caveman** ⚔️ — The versatile frontline unit. Cheap at 2 gold with no tech requirement, making them the backbone of any army early on.

**Archer** 🏹 — Ranged attacker with 2-tile range. Strikes from behind friendly lines and avoids [counter-attacks](/wiki/units/combat) against melee defenders entirely. Requires Archer.

**Guard** 🛡️ — The tankiest general unit with high HP and defense. Cannot attack after moving — must be in ready mode to fight. Ideal for holding villages and key positions. Requires Guard.

**Spearman** 🔱 — Anti-cavalry specialist. Brace passive punishes Seers, Sprinters, and Tidebreakers with 1.5x counter-attack damage. Requires Spearman.

**Seer** 🐎 — Fast melee unit with 2 movement and 2 exploration range. After attacking and surviving, it enters retreat mode: one follow-up move is available, then the unit is spent. With Scout/Healer culled, the Seer is now the workhorse of early scouting.

**Sprinter** ⚜️ — Elite cavalry. Charge adds +5 ATK when attacking after moving, and a kill leaves the Sprinter able to attack again without moving again. Devastating on the offense but expensive. Requires Sprinter.

**Mage** 💥 — Siege unit with long range, heavy damage, and reduced splash damage to adjacent enemies. Cannot attack after moving, so positioning matters. Requires Mage (formerly Engineering).

**Swordsman** 🗡️ — Heavy infantry unlocked by Swordsman (formerly Smithing). Strong against melee defenders and built to contest fortified ground.

**Bombardier** 🧨 — Water-branch ranged unit unlocked by Water & Bomber. Becomes a tougher bombship-style unit on water with stronger splash attacks. Has innate ocean traversal — no Ocean & Admiral required.

**Tidebreaker** ⚓ — Deep-water unit unlocked by Ocean & Admiral. Becomes a battleship-style unit on water with strong naval combat stats and the **Tide Command** passive (attack after moving onto water). Cavalry-class — vulnerable to Spearman Brace.

## Hero Units

Each [tribe](/wiki/tribes/all-tribes) has a unique Hero unit (shown above in the Heroes section). Heroes are powerful units with fixed stats — they do **not** level up through play.

### Summoning a Hero

A Hero is **not** trainable with gold. The owning Player summons their Tribe's Hero by choosing the `special_unit` [Village Perk](/wiki/buildings/villages#village-perks) when one of their Villages levels up to **level 5**. The perk consumes that level-up's choice and is the only level at which `special_unit` is offered.

- If a Player picks any other Perk at the level-5 transition, that Village will never spawn a Hero.
- Captured Villages keep the Perks chosen by their previous owner. A captor cannot retroactively summon a Hero from a captured level-5+ Village.
- Only **one Hero** can exist on the board per Tribe at a time. If your Hero dies, you can spend a future level-5 Perk slot from another Village to summon a new one.

### Hero Death

When a Hero is killed, all other units of that Tribe receive a **morale loss** debuff (−1 ATK for 2 turns). Protect your Hero — their death weakens your entire army.

## Veterancy

All units gain veterancy bonuses through kills. Every 2 kills, a unit receives an alternating bonus:

- **2 kills** → +1 ATK
- **4 kills** → +2 max HP
- **6 kills** → +1 ATK
- And so on, alternating between ATK and HP bonuses

Heroes do **not** gain veterancy or kill-based stat tiers — their stats are fixed.
