## Training Units

Units are trained at [Villages](/wiki/buildings/villages) you own. To train a unit:

1. Select a Village tile that belongs to your Tribe
2. The Village must **not** have a Unit on it already
3. The Village must have room in its **population cap**
4. You must have enough **gold** to cover the training cost
5. For tech-gated units, the required [technology](/wiki/mechanics/technology) must be researched

[Heroes](/wiki/units/unit-types#hero-units) cannot be trained — they are summoned only via the `special_unit` [Village Perk](/wiki/buildings/villages#village-perks) at level 5.

### Population

Each trained unit increases the Village's population by 1. The population cap depends on the [Village level](/wiki/buildings/villages) — a level 1 Village can support 2 units (3 if it is your [Capital](/wiki/buildings/villages#capital)). If the Village is at max population, you cannot train more units there.

Newly trained units start in **ready** mode, meaning they can act immediately on the same turn they are trained.

## Healing Units

Damaged units have two ways to recover health.

### Heal at a Village (1 gold)

Bringing a damaged unit onto a friendly tile within your territory and using **Heal**:

1. Select a tile with your damaged unit
2. The unit must be in **ready** mode (hasn't moved or acted)
3. You must have at least **1 gold**

Healing costs **1 gold** and restores the unit to **full health** instantly. After healing, the unit's mode is set to **acted**.

### Recover (self-heal, free)

Any unit can use the **Recover** action on itself to heal a portion of its missing HP without spending gold. Recover is the replacement for the dropped Healer's heal-ally action — every unit now has a baseline self-heal:

- The unit must be in **ready** mode
- Recovers a fixed amount of HP (capped at max HP)
- Sets the unit to **acted** mode after use
- Costs no gold

Recover is the right choice when you want to nurse a frontline unit between engagements without retreating to a Village or paying a heal fee.

## Retiring Units

Once you research **Market**, you can retire your own non-Hero units that are still in **ready** mode. Retiring removes the unit from the board and credits half its training cost back to your treasury (rounded down, minimum 1 gold). Hero units cannot be retired.

Retire is useful for clearing population for a higher-tier unit, repositioning your army's composition, or recovering some gold from a unit that has outlived its strategic role.

## Heal vs. Replace

| Factor       | Heal           | Recover            | Train new unit    |
| ------------ | -------------- | ------------------ | ----------------- |
| Cost         | 1 gold         | Free               | 2–7 gold          |
| Population   | No change      | No change          | +1 to Village     |
| Availability | Friendly tile  | Anywhere, any unit | Village only      |
| Turn cost    | Uses full turn | Uses full turn     | Ready immediately |

Recover is cheapest but partial. Heal is full restore but costs gold and ties the unit to your territory. Training a replacement is a last resort once population has the room.
