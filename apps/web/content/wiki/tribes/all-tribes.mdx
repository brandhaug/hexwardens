Hexwardens features 9 distinct Tribes, each with a unique home [terrain](/wiki/getting-started/terrain), color, starting unit composition, [Hero unit](/wiki/units/unit-types), and [technology](/wiki/mechanics/technology) affinities. All Tribes begin with **5 gold** in the treasury and one **[Capital](/wiki/buildings/villages#capital)** Village.

Every Tribe starts with a **Caveman** on its [Capital](/wiki/buildings/villages#capital) Village tile and (where listed) a secondary Unit on an adjacent tile. With Scout and Shaman both culled, **Wraiths** and **Skeletons** no longer get a tier-2 starting Unit and instead rely on early tech to round out their opening.

## The Capital

Each Tribe's starting Village is its **Capital** — the only Village that grants the Tribe a **+1 maximum population** bonus at every level. The Capital flag survives capture, and a Tribe whose Capital is taken does not gain a new Capital from any other Village. Push your Capital toward level 5 — that is the only Village transition where you can summon your Hero (see below).

## Notable Differences

- **Barbarians** are the only Tribe that starts with two Cavemen, giving them early combat strength.
- **Rogues** start with a [Seer](/wiki/units/unit-types), providing early scouting and mobility.
- **Druids** and **Frostkin** start with a Spearman, giving them early anti-cavalry defense.
- **Orcs** and **Engineers** start with a Guard, offering strong early-game Village defense.
- **Wraiths** and **Skeletons** start with no secondary Unit; their early game leans on Caveman-plus-tech tempo.

## Hero Units

Each Tribe has a unique [Hero unit](/wiki/units/unit-types#hero-units). Heroes have **fixed stats** — they do not level up through play.

A Hero is **not** trainable with gold. The owning Player summons their Tribe's Hero by choosing the `special_unit` [Village Perk](/wiki/buildings/villages#village-perks) when one of their Villages levels up to **level 5**. The perk consumes that level-up's choice and is the only level at which `special_unit` is offered. Captured Villages keep prior owners' Perk choices, so a captor cannot retroactively summon a Hero from a captured level-5+ Village. See [ADR-0001](https://github.com/brandhaug/hexwardens/blob/master/docs/adr/0001-hero-as-level-up-perk.md) for the rationale.

## Home Terrain

Each Tribe's starting Village is placed on its home terrain during [board generation](/wiki/getting-started/terrain). The surrounding area will contain resources matching that terrain type, giving each Tribe natural access to specific resource types.

## Tribe Elimination

A Tribe is eliminated when it loses its **last Village** — typically when an enemy [Capture flips](/wiki/mechanics/capture) the Tribe's final holding. When eliminated:

- All remaining Units of that Tribe are removed from the board.
- The eliminated Tribe takes no further Turns.

When a Tribe's [Hero](/wiki/units/unit-types) is killed, all other Units of that Tribe receive a morale-loss debuff (−1 ATK for 2 turns).

## Tribe Colors

Each Tribe has a distinctive color used for Villages, Units, and UI elements. Colors help identify ownership at a glance across the hex grid.
