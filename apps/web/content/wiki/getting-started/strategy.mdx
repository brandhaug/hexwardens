This guide covers fundamental strategies to help you win your first Matches of Hexwardens.

## Early Game (Rounds 1–5)

Your opening Turns set the tone for the rest of the Match. Prioritize:

1. **Scout with your secondary Unit** — Move your starting secondary Unit (or a Seer if your Tribe has one) outward to reveal the map and locate [ruins](/wiki/mechanics/ruins), enemy Villages, and resource clusters.
2. **Explore ruins immediately** — A 10-gold ruin reward funds an entire opening. Bring a Unit to a ruin one round, explore the next.
3. **Collect resources near your Capital** — At 2 gold and 1 XP each, resources are the cheapest way to level your starting Village.
4. **Research a basic Path tech** — Basic-tier techs are instant and cheap. They unlock harvesting or unit access on the relevant [Tech Path](/wiki/mechanics/technology#tech-paths).
5. **Build a primary structure** — A single primary build gives 5 XP, enough to jump a level 1 Village to level 2 (threshold: 2 XP) and beyond.

## Capital Development

Your **[Capital](/wiki/buildings/villages#capital)** is special — it has +1 max population at every level, and it is the only Village guaranteed to be in your hands long enough to chase the level-5 [Hero summon Perk](/wiki/buildings/villages#village-perks).

- **Don't ignore the Capital's economy.** Cluster your earliest builds and collections inside its control range.
- **Push for level 5 early.** That is the only level where the `special_unit` Perk is offered. Miss it and that Village can never spawn a Hero.
- **A captured Capital does not "promote" any other Village.** Each Tribe has exactly one Capital per Match. Defending it is a strategic priority.

## Capture Timing

Capture is a **two-step Action** (see [Capture](/wiki/mechanics/capture)). The capturing Unit is locked to `acted` on the Village tile until your next Turn, when ownership flips. Plan around this:

- **Pre-position a defender** before issuing capture. The capturing Unit cannot defend itself for a full Turn.
- **A lone Seer cannot solo a contested capture.** It will sit exposed for a full Turn before ownership flips — a single counter-attack can cancel the capture entirely.
- **Use the setup window offensively too.** A Mage or Archer one tile away can punish enemies trying to break your pending capture.

## Economy Management

Gold is your most important resource. Every action costs gold, so balancing income and spending is critical.

- **Salary scales with Village level.** A level 1 Village pays 1 gold; a level 5 Village pays 5 gold. Leveling Villages is the best long-term investment.
- **Per-Village research scaling** charges +1 gold per owned Village beyond the first. A 5-Village empire pays +4 on every research — territory has a hidden tech tax.
- **Recover is free.** Use the [Recover](/wiki/units/training-and-healing#recover-self-heal-free) self-heal action between engagements rather than spending 1 gold on Heal whenever you can.
- **Heal at home** when you need a full restore — 1 gold for a complete top-off is still cheap.
- **Don't over-train.** A few well-positioned Units outperform a swarm you can't afford to maintain.

## Research Strategy

[Technology](/wiki/mechanics/technology) is structured as **six Paths** (Mobility + five elemental Paths). Each elemental Path follows the shape `basic → econ secondary → military secondary → Mastery`, with the Mastery unlocking a **Large** building upgrade.

- **Basic-tier techs are free actions** — they complete instantly, so research one early.
- **Masteries take a full Turn**, blocking other research for that Turn. Time them for a quiet Turn.
- **A Mastery is two payoffs in one** — passive buff plus the Large upgrade for that Path's secondary building. That makes Path commitment more attractive than spreading across all five.
- **Mobility Path** is purely utility. Climbing, Road & Seaports, Market, Mobility Mastery — the Mastery's `+1 movement to all units` is one of the strongest blanket passives.

## Village Leveling

[Villages](/wiki/buildings/villages) are your economic engine. Focus on leveling them quickly:

| Action           | XP   | Cost   | Efficiency |
| ---------------- | ---- | ------ | ---------- |
| Collect resource | 1 XP | 2 gold | Best value |
| Build structure  | 5 XP | 5 gold | Fastest    |
| Explore ruins    | 3 XP | —      | One-shot   |

- Level 1 → 2 requires only **2 XP** (one build does it instantly).
- A level 5 Village generates 5 gold/Turn and is the target for the Hero-summon Perk.
- **Capital Villages always sit one population step ahead** — keep your high-tier units fed by upgrading the Capital first.

## Combat Tactics

- **Use ranged units from 2+ tiles away** to avoid counter-attacks against melee defenders.
- **Soften targets first** — Wounded units deal less [damage](/wiki/units/combat), so chipping health before a melee engage tips the fight in your favor.
- **Guards hold positions** — High HP and defense make them extremely durable on Village tiles.
- **Seers and Sprinters raid** — Use their mobility for hit-and-run attacks. With Scout removed, the Seer is your primary scout too.
- **Spearmen counter cavalry** — Brace deals 1.5x damage when counter-attacking Seers, Sprinters, and Tidebreakers.
- **Lead with Mages** — Long range plus splash softens clustered enemies before your melee line engages.

## Hero Perk Choice

Reaching a level-5 Village forces a long-term decision: take the `special_unit` Perk to summon your [Hero](/wiki/units/unit-types#hero-units), or take an economic / territorial Perk and forgo a Hero from that Village.

- A Hero is a fixed-stat tribe flagship. There is no kill-leveling — what you summon is what you get.
- Only **one Hero** can exist on the board per Tribe at a time. Death debuffs the entire Tribe (−1 ATK morale loss, 2 turns).
- If your first level-5 Village is a soft target, consider taking economic Perks there and saving `special_unit` for a safer level-5 Village down the line. But remember: every Village you skip the Perk on is one fewer chance to ever summon your Hero.

## Common Mistakes

- **Capturing without backup** — The Unit on the Village tile is exposed for a full Turn. A single counter-attack cancels the capture.
- **Ignoring ruins** — The 10 gold reward is game-changing in the early rounds.
- **Skipping basic techs** — Path basics are instant and cheap. Don't delay.
- **Over-expanding before consolidating** — Per-Village research scaling penalizes width without depth. Three level-5 Villages beat seven level-2 ones.
- **Picking the wrong Perk at level 5** — Once you take a non-`special_unit` Perk at the level-5 transition, that Village can never summon a Hero, regardless of how often you level it.
- **Leaving Villages undefended** — A single enemy Unit can issue capture; if the setup window expires unchallenged, ownership flips. Always keep a defender within reach.
