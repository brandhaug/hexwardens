When starting a new Match, you configure your game through a setup screen.

## Basic Options

### Tribe

Choose from 9 playable [Tribes](/wiki/tribes/all-tribes). Each Tribe starts on its preferred terrain type and begins with a Caveman and (where applicable) a tribe-specific secondary Unit.

### Opponents

Select between 1 and 7 [Bot](/wiki/tribes/all-tribes) opponents. More opponents creates a larger map with more Villages, resources, and strategic complexity.

### Difficulty

Five difficulty levels are available: **Beginner**, **Intermediate**, **Advanced**, **Expert**, and **Legend**.

## Advanced Options

These scale automatically with the number of opponents but can be manually adjusted:

| Setting           | Range   | Default scaling     |
| ----------------- | ------- | ------------------- |
| Villages          | 6–24    | ~3 per player       |
| Tiles             | 127–469 | Scales with players |
| Resources         | 24–72   | ~4 per Village      |
| Ruins             | 2–8     | ~1 per player       |
| Primary Buildings | 12–36   | ~2 per Village      |

More tiles means a larger hex grid. More resources and buildings means more opportunities to [level up Villages](/wiki/buildings/villages) quickly.

## Starting Conditions

Every Tribe begins with:

- **5 gold** in the treasury
- **1 [Capital](/wiki/buildings/villages#capital) Village** on its preferred [terrain](/wiki/getting-started/terrain), flagged as Capital (+1 max population at every level)
- A **Caveman** on the Capital tile
- A **secondary Unit** on an adjacent tile (varies by Tribe — see [Tribes](/wiki/tribes/all-tribes); Wraiths and Skeletons start without a secondary Unit)
- The area around the Capital already explored
