Utility buildings are non-economic structures unlocked by the linear **Mobility** [Tech Path](/wiki/mechanics/technology). They support unit movement, naval transit, and the gold economy rather than feeding [Village](/wiki/buildings/villages) experience.

| Utility | Tech Path Step  | Function                                                                                                |
| ------- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Road    | Road & Seaports | Reduces movement cost on the connected tile                                                             |
| Seaport | Road & Seaports | Embarkation point that lets land units cross [shallow water](/wiki/getting-started/terrain)             |
| Market  | Market          | Enables [Retire](/wiki/units/training-and-healing#retiring-units) and unlocks the Mobility Mastery tier |

Roads, Seaports, and Markets are declared as a separate `UtilityBuilding` Tile item — they are not built on top of the elemental primary/secondary chain and do not award the same +5 XP per build that economic buildings do.

## Mobility Path Recap

The four-node Mobility Path is:

1. **Climbing** — opens stone traversal.
2. **Road & Seaports** — declares Roads and Seaports as buildable utilities.
3. **Market** — declares Markets and unlocks the Retire action on non-Hero units.
4. **Mobility Mastery** — passive: +1 movement to all units.
