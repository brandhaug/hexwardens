The building roster is structured around the five elemental [Tech Paths](/wiki/mechanics/technology) (Water, Plants, Earth, Fire, Mineral). Each Path has a **primary** (tier-1) building, a **secondary** (tier-2) building, and a **Large** upgrade unlocked at the Path's [Mastery](/wiki/mechanics/technology#masteries-and-large-upgrades).

Primary buildings are placed during board generation and start in an **idle** state — a Player builds them once their territory reaches the tile.

## Building Cost

All primary buildings cost **5 gold** to build. Building awards **5 XP** to the controlling [Village](/wiki/buildings/villages).

## Requirements

- The building must be within your **Village control range**
- The building must be in **idle** (unbuilt) state
- You must have **5 gold**

## Primary Building List

| Building    | Tech Path | Terrain | Secondary Building                                |
| ----------- | --------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Tavern      | Water     | Water   | [Dock](/wiki/buildings/secondary-buildings)       |
| Farm        | Plants    | Grass   | [Windmill](/wiki/buildings/secondary-buildings)   |
| Lumber Mill | Earth     | Forest  | [Watermill](/wiki/buildings/secondary-buildings)  |
| Camp        | Fire      | Desert  | [Stables](/wiki/buildings/secondary-buildings)    |
| Mine        | Mineral   | Stone   | [Blacksmith](/wiki/buildings/secondary-buildings) |

The 12 visual [terrain](/wiki/getting-started/terrain) types are mapped onto the five Path families, so each Path always has a primary that can spawn somewhere on a generated map even when the lead terrain is scarce.

Primary buildings are placed within **2 hexes** of a Village during [board generation](/wiki/getting-started/terrain), so they are always reachable once you control a nearby Village.

## Strategic Value

Primary buildings serve three purposes:

1. **Village XP** — The 5 XP from building is the largest single XP source, often enough to trigger a [Village level-up](/wiki/buildings/villages#leveling) at low levels.
2. **[Secondary buildings](/wiki/buildings/secondary-buildings)** — Each primary unlocks a corresponding secondary that can be constructed adjacent to it.
3. **Large upgrades** — Once you research a Path's [Mastery](/wiki/mechanics/technology#masteries-and-large-upgrades), built secondaries can be upgraded to their **Large** form for another burst of XP.

Building structures is one of the fastest ways to [level up Villages](/wiki/buildings/villages) — a single build at a level 1 Village (which needs only 2 XP) triggers an immediate level-up with XP to spare.
