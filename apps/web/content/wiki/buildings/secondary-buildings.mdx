Secondary buildings are tier-2 structures built **adjacent to** a corresponding [primary building](/wiki/buildings/primary-buildings). They provide additional Village experience and unlock the Path's **Large** upgrade once its [Mastery](/wiki/mechanics/technology#masteries-and-large-upgrades) is researched.

## Building Requirements

- The tile must be **empty** (no existing item or unit)
- The tile must be **adjacent** to a built primary building you own
- The tile must be within your **Village control range**
- The primary building must not already support a secondary building of that type
- You must have **5 gold**

## Cost and Rewards

Secondary buildings cost **5 gold** and award **5 Village XP** to the controlling Village.

## Secondary Building List

| Secondary Building | Primary Building | Tech Path | Terrain |
| ------------------ | ---------------- | --------- | ------- |
| Dock               | Tavern           | Water     | Water   |
| Windmill           | Farm             | Plants    | Grass   |
| Watermill          | Lumber Mill      | Earth     | Forest  |
| Stables            | Camp             | Fire      | Desert  |
| Blacksmith         | Mine             | Mineral   | Stone   |

## Large Upgrades

Researching a Path's **Mastery** unlocks a one-time `upgrade-to-large` action against any of that Path's built secondaries. The upgrade costs **5 gold** and awards another **5 XP**.

| Path    | Mastery Tech    | Secondary  | Large Upgrade    |
| ------- | --------------- | ---------- | ---------------- |
| Water   | Water Mastery   | Dock       | Large Dock       |
| Plants  | Plant Mastery   | Windmill   | Large Windmill   |
| Earth   | Earth Mastery   | Watermill  | Large Watermill  |
| Fire    | Fire Mastery    | Stables    | Large Stables    |
| Mineral | Mineral Mastery | Blacksmith | Large Blacksmith |

A Large building is a final state — it cannot be upgraded further or downgraded, and the action is denied if the secondary is already Large.

## Placement Rules

Each primary building can only support **one** secondary. You cannot build multiple Stables around the same Camp. However, if you control multiple primaries of the same type, each can support its own secondary.

## Strategic Tips

- A primary + secondary pair costs **10 gold** and yields **10 XP** — one of the most efficient ways to level a Village.
- Researching a Mastery extends that Path's chain to **15 XP** through the Large upgrade, making Mastery an economic milestone as well as a military one.
- Plan placement carefully: secondary builds need an **empty adjacent tile**, so don't block all six neighbors of a primary with other items.
