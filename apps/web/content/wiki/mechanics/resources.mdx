Resources are collectible items scattered across the map. Gathering them earns Village experience and clears the way for [buildings](/wiki/buildings/primary-buildings).

## Collecting Resources

To collect a resource:

1. The resource must be within your **[Village control range](/wiki/buildings/villages)**
2. You must have at least **2 gold**
3. Some resources require a **harvesting [technology](/wiki/mechanics/technology)** before they can be collected

Collecting costs **2 gold** and awards **1 XP** to the controlling Village. The resource is removed from the map after collection.

### Gated Resources

Several resources require their basic-tier Tech Path harvesting tech before they can be collected. Examples include the Water-Path Pearl tech, the Fire-Path Cheese tech, and the Mineral-Path Gem tech. These basic techs are tier-1, instant, and cheap — research them early if your starting territory contains the matching resource.

Resources are placed within 2 hexes of a Village during board generation, ensuring they are always reachable once you control the nearby Village.

## Strategic Tips

- At **2 gold** per collection, resources are the cheapest way to gain Village XP.
- Prioritize collecting near low-level Villages — a level 1 Village only needs **2 XP** to level up, so two collections trigger a level-up.
- Grass terrain spawns two resource types (Apple and Wheat), making it especially valuable territory.
- Per-Village research scaling means a small empire of well-fed Villages can be cheaper to research from than a sprawl of underdeveloped ones.
