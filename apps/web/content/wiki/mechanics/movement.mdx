Units move across the hex grid tile by tile. Each [unit type](/wiki/units/unit-types) has a movement stat that determines how far it can travel per Turn.

## Movement Points

| Unit        | Land Movement | Water Movement |
| ----------- | ------------- | -------------- |
| Caveman     | 1             | 1              |
| Archer      | 1             | 1              |
| Guard       | 1             | 1              |
| Spearman    | 1             | 1              |
| Seer        | 2             | 1              |
| Sprinter    | 3             | 1              |
| Mage        | 1             | 1              |
| Swordsman   | 1             | 1              |
| Bombardier  | 1             | 2              |
| Tidebreaker | 1             | 3              |

**Seers** and **Sprinters** carry the lion's share of land mobility. With Scout culled, the Seer is the workhorse for early exploration.

[Hero units](/wiki/units/unit-types) generally have 1 movement, with notable exceptions: **Wolf** and **Skeleton Golem** have 2 movement.

## Movement Rules

- A unit must be in **ready** mode to move
- Units cannot move onto tiles occupied by other units
- Movement uses breadth-first pathfinding to find valid destinations
- After moving, the unit enters **moved** mode
- A Seer in **retreat** mode can take exactly one follow-up move (no further attack this round)
- The [Frostlord's](/wiki/units/unit-types) Frozen Ground ability reduces enemy movement by 1 within 1 tile

### Terrain Costs

Most tiles cost 1 movement to enter. Exceptions:

- **Stone tiles** require **Climbing** tech to enter at all, and cost **2 movement** per step (so a 1-movement unit cannot reach a stone tile without a movement bonus).
- **Shallow water** requires **Water & Bomber** for ordinary land units. Native water-branch units (Bombardier, Tidebreaker) ignore the gate.
- **Deep ocean** requires **Ocean & Admiral** (which carries the `ocean_traversal` passive). Bombardier and Tidebreaker have innate ocean traversal — no tech needed.

## Water vs. Ocean

The board now distinguishes two water tiers:

- **Shallow water** lines coastlines and accepts Tavern / Dock placement; it is gated by **Water & Bomber**.
- **Deep ocean** is interior water surrounded entirely by water; it is gated by **Ocean & Admiral**.

Once on water, a land unit fights and moves as a [naval form](#naval-forms) of itself. **Bombardiers** become tougher bombship-style units with stronger splash; **Tidebreakers** become fast battleship-style units (3 water movement, 2 range).

### Naval forms

Land units cross water with their water-form stats: typically 1–2 water movement, naval range, and the ability to keep attacking after embarking.

## Flying

The **flying** [passive](/wiki/units/unit-types#passive-abilities) is wired into the engine but no production unit currently carries it. When a unit gains flying:

- **In transit**, every tile costs a flat 1 movement and tech gates are ignored. The unit can pass over stone, water, and ocean freely without Climbing or the water techs.
- **On the destination tile**, the same gates as ground movement apply: Climbing for stone, Water & Bomber for shallow water, Ocean & Admiral for deep ocean. A flying unit cannot end its Turn on a tile its Tribe could not normally enter.
- The movement budget always uses the unit's `movement` stat — never `waterMovement` — even when starting on water.
- Flying units gain **+1 sight** and default to attacking after moving.

## After Moving

Once a unit has moved, it enters **moved** mode. What it can do next depends on the unit type:

| Unit        | Attack After Move |
| ----------- | ----------------- |
| Caveman     | Yes               |
| Archer      | Yes               |
| Guard       | No                |
| Spearman    | Yes               |
| Seer        | Yes               |
| Sprinter    | Yes               |
| Mage        | No                |
| Swordsman   | Yes               |
| Bombardier  | Yes               |
| Tidebreaker | Yes               |

Guards and Mages cannot attack after moving — plan their positioning carefully.

No unit can [capture](/wiki/mechanics/capture), [explore ruins](/wiki/mechanics/ruins), or [heal](/wiki/units/training-and-healing) after moving. These actions all require the unit to be in **ready** mode.

## Exploration Range

Moving a unit reveals nearby tiles based on its exploration range:

| Unit       | Exploration Range |
| ---------- | ----------------- |
| Seer       | 2 tiles           |
| Sprinter   | 2 tiles           |
| Most units | 1 tile            |

Seers and Sprinters are the best explorers — their 2 movement combined with 2 exploration range covers ground fast. See [Visibility](/wiki/mechanics/visibility) for more on fog of war, including the +1 sight on stone tiles and for flying units.
