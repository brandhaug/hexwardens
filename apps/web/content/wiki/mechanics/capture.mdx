[Villages](/wiki/buildings/villages) are taken by **Capture**, a two-step Action that locks the capturing Unit on the Village tile for one Turn before ownership flips.

## The Two Steps

### Step 1 — Setup

A Player issues `capture` on one of their Units that is standing on the enemy or neutral Village tile and is in **ready** mode. On dispatch:

- The Unit's mode is set to **acted** for the rest of this Turn (it cannot move, attack, or capture again).
- The Village gains a `captureSetup` marker: `{ byTribe, turnInitiated }`.
- Ownership does **not** flip yet. The Village still belongs to the previous owner — the marker simply records that a capture is in progress.

The marker is publicly visible — every Player can see that a capture is being attempted.

### Step 2 — Flip

At the start of the capturing Tribe's **next Turn**, the turn pipeline runs a `captureFlip` phase. For each Village with a `captureSetup`:

- If the same Tribe's Unit still occupies the tile, ownership flips. The Village's `tribe` and `state` change; level, Perks, Capital flag, experience, and population structure are preserved (population resets to 0). A `villageCaptured` event is broadcast.
- If the Unit moved off, was killed, or an enemy Unit now stands on the tile, the marker is cleared **without** flipping ownership.

The Village's perks and level survive the flip. A captured level-5+ Village does **not** let the captor pick `special_unit` retroactively — the previous owner's Perk choices are inherited.

## Cancelling a Capture

Three things cancel an in-progress capture:

1. **The capturing Unit moves away.** The marker is cleared at flip time when no friendly Unit remains on the tile.
2. **The capturing Unit is killed.** Same effect — no Unit, no flip.
3. **An enemy Unit recaptures the tile** (e.g., the previous owner walks a defender onto the Village before the capturing Tribe's next Turn). The marker is cleared without flipping.

Concurrent attempts: if two enemy Units somehow contest the Village in the same setup window, the most recent `capture` Action overwrites any prior marker. Ownership flips for whichever Tribe holds the Unit at the start of the next applicable Turn.

## Tribe Elimination

When a flip removes the previous owner's **last Village**, that Tribe is **eliminated**. All of the Tribe's remaining units are removed from the board, and the Tribe takes no further Turns.

The 500-Turn cap is retained as a draw safety valve; if it expires with more than one Tribe still alive, the Match ends as a draw with no winner declared.

## Strategic Implications

- The capturing Unit is **exposed** through the entire setup window. Plan a follow-up defender for the tile before issuing capture.
- A single fast Unit (Seer, Sprinter) cannot solo-capture a contested Village — it gets one Turn of vulnerability before ownership flips.
- Use the setup window offensively too: a Mage parked one tile away can punish anyone who tries to break the capture.
