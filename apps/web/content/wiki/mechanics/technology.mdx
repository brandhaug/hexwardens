Tech research opens unit training, terrain interactions, building upgrades, and Mastery passives. The tree is structured as **six [Tech Paths](/wiki/mechanics/technology#tech-paths)** in line with Hexwardens' canonical shape.

## Research

Research is performed at [Villages](/wiki/buildings/villages) you own. To start researching:

1. Select a Village tile that belongs to your Tribe
2. Choose an available technology from the research menu
3. You must have enough **gold** to cover the cost
4. Only **one technology** can be researched at a time per Player

Most technologies complete instantly. **Masteries** take a full Turn to research, and you cannot start any other research while a Mastery is in progress — the cost is the opportunity, not just the gold. Research progress ticks down by 1 at the end of each of your Turns.

### Per-Village research cost scaling

Research cost is the node's base cost **plus +1 gold for every Village a Tribe owns beyond the first**. Expanding gives more income and training sites, but it also makes future research progressively more expensive — every Village is a step on the cost curve. The scaling encourages players to weigh territory expansion against research efficiency, and it is the main reason why two-Village pushes can be more economical than chain-conquest sprawl.

## Tech Paths

There are six Paths.

### Mobility (utility, four linear nodes)

`Climbing → Road & Seaports → Market → Mobility Mastery`

The Mobility Path is purely utility. It opens stone traversal, declares the Roads / Seaports / Market [utility buildings](/wiki/buildings/utility-buildings), and ends in **Mobility Mastery**, a passive that grants **+1 movement to all units**.

### The five elemental Paths

Each elemental Path follows the same shape: **basic Tech → economic-secondary branch → military-secondary branch → Mastery tip**.

| Path    | Basic  | Econ Secondary          | Military Secondary               | Mastery         |
| ------- | ------ | ----------------------- | -------------------------------- | --------------- |
| Water   | Pearl  | Coral / Dock            | Water & Bomber → Ocean & Admiral | Water Mastery   |
| Plants  | Fruit  | Farming → Windmill      | Guard → City Wall                | Plant Mastery   |
| Earth   | Meat   | Lumber Mill → Watermill | Archer → Mage                    | Earth Mastery   |
| Fire    | Cheese | Camp → Stables          | Seer → Sprinter                  | Fire Mastery    |
| Mineral | Gem    | Mine → Blacksmith       | Spearman → Swordsman             | Mineral Mastery |

The five elemental Paths align 1:1 with the [primary/secondary buildings](/wiki/buildings/primary-buildings) and the [Large upgrades](#masteries-and-large-upgrades).

## Masteries and Large Upgrades

Each Mastery grants a passive buff and unlocks the corresponding **Large** upgrade for the Path's economic-tier-2 building (see [secondary buildings](/wiki/buildings/secondary-buildings#large-upgrades)). Mobility Mastery is the only Mastery with no Large upgrade — its payoff is the +1 movement passive.

| Mastery          | Passive (sample)          | Large Upgrade    |
| ---------------- | ------------------------- | ---------------- |
| Mobility Mastery | +1 movement to all units  | —                |
| Water Mastery    | Water-branch buffs        | Large Dock       |
| Plant Mastery    | Plant-branch buffs        | Large Windmill   |
| Earth Mastery    | +1 sight for ranged units | Large Watermill  |
| Fire Mastery     | Fire-branch buffs         | Large Stables    |
| Mineral Mastery  | Mineral-branch buffs      | Large Blacksmith |

## Unit Unlocks

Many technologies unlock new [unit types](/wiki/units/unit-types) for training:

| Technology       | Unlocks     |
| ---------------- | ----------- |
| Archer           | Archer      |
| Guard            | Guard       |
| Seer             | Seer        |
| Spearman         | Spearman    |
| Sprinter (tech)  | Sprinter    |
| Mage (tech)      | Mage        |
| Swordsman (tech) | Swordsman   |
| Water & Bomber   | Bombardier  |
| Ocean & Admiral  | Tidebreaker |

Cavemen are available from the start without any technology.

The previously available **Healer**, **Shaman**, **Giant**, and **Scout** units are removed. The Healer's role is filled by the [Recover](/wiki/units/training-and-healing#recover-self-heal-free) self-heal action available to every Unit; the Scout's exploration role moves to the Seer.

## Defensive Unlocks

City Wall is a Plants-Path defensive technology. Once researched, units defending one of your Village tiles gain **+5 DEF**.

## Tribe-Aligned Technologies

Each Tribe has technologies that align with its strengths. Researching an aligned tech awards a small bonus.

## Hero Summoning

Heroes are **not** unlocked through tech. They are summoned only by choosing the `special_unit` [Village Perk](/wiki/buildings/villages#village-perks) at the level-5 transition of a Village you own — see [ADR-0001](https://github.com/brandhaug/hexwardens/blob/master/docs/adr/0001-hero-as-level-up-perk.md) for the design rationale.

## Strategic Considerations

The branching tree forces meaningful choices — you cannot rush everything at once. Key trade-offs:

- **Opening basic techs are cheap and instant** — there's no reason not to take one early.
- **Per-Village scaling means territory has a hidden tech tax.** A 5-Village empire pays +4 gold on every research; budget accordingly.
- **Masteries take a full Turn.** Time them for a quiet Turn — you give up all other research that Turn.
- **Large upgrades double back into Path Masteries.** A Mastery is both a passive buff and a building unlock, so two Path Masteries unlock 10 XP of secondary growth.
