Ruins (🏛️) are ancient structures scattered across the map that yield random rewards when explored.

## Exploring Ruins

To explore ruins, you need a [Unit](/wiki/units/unit-types) on the same tile. The Unit must be in **ready** mode — it **cannot** explore after moving or acting.

Exploring grants one random reward and removes the ruins from the map. The Unit's mode is set to **acted** after exploring.

## Rewards

When you explore ruins, you receive one of three equally likely rewards:

### Gold

You find **10 gold** added to your treasury — a major early-game windfall that can fund several units or buildings.

### Experience

The nearest controlling [Village](/wiki/buildings/villages) gains **3 XP**. This can trigger an instant level-up at low levels (a level 1 Village only needs 2 XP). If no Village controls the ruins tile, the experience is lost.

### Treasure

You find treasure worth **5 gold** added to your treasury.

## Strategic Tips

- Ruins are **high-priority early targets** — the 10 gold reward can fund a strong early army.
- Send [Seers](/wiki/units/unit-types) or [Sprinters](/wiki/units/unit-types) to reach ruins first — their 2–3 movement covers ground fast.
- Units must be in **ready** mode to explore, so move a Unit onto a ruins tile one round, then explore the next.
- The 3 XP Village reward can jump a level 1 Village straight to level 2 (threshold: 2 XP), giving you an early economic edge.
